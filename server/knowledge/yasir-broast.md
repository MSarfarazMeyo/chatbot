# Yasir Broast — Business Knowledge

## About
Yasir Broast is a Pakistani fast-food chain known for crispy broast (fried)
chicken, burgers, rolls and family deals. Founded in Lahore, with branches
across Punjab. All meat is halal. No pork or non-halal items are served.

## Branches & timings
- Johar Town, Lahore — 12:00 PM to 2:00 AM daily
- Gulberg, Lahore — 12:00 PM to 1:00 AM daily
- Bahria Town, Rawalpindi — 1:00 PM to 2:00 AM daily
- Multan Road, Lahore — 12:00 PM to 1:00 AM daily

- **Student Deal:** Zinger Burger + loaded fries + small drink — **Rs. 690** (valid 12 PM–4 PM, student card required)
- **Midnight Deal:** 4-piece broast + fries + drink — **Rs. 650** (after 11 PM)

## Delivery
- Delivery available via the Yasir Broast website, phone and foodpanda.
- Delivery fee: Rs. 150 within 5 km; **free over Rs. 2,000**.
- Average delivery time: 30–45 minutes.

## Policies
- Prices include tax. No extra charges at checkout.
- Refunds/exchange for wrong or missing items within 1 hour of delivery — call the branch.
- Spice levels: mild, hot, extra hot (broast can be requested at any level).
- Advance orders for events (10+ pieces) accepted with 2 hours notice.

## FAQ
- **Q: Is everything halal?** A: Yes, 100% halal chicken, no pork anywhere.
- **Q: Do you deliver at 2 AM?** A: Yes, until closing time at branches open till 2 AM.
- **Q: Can I pay by card?** A: Card accepted at branches; delivery is cash on delivery only.
- **Q: Do you have vegetarian options?** A: Only fries, loaded fries and drinks. No vegetarian main dishes.
